# Decisions

Newest first. D-### are my design calls where BRIEF.md is silent; U-### are director's notes.

- U-007 Director: "I like how the current default hand position is for when the player is in the air/doing parkour stuff. However, when the player is on the ground or running... they need different positions that look more like walking or running and also it shouldn't show so much sleeve... that default hand position is fine for crawling. When the player is holding an item they shouldn't be able to grab onto stuff with their hands, and also items should always be held with two hands (unless it's really small)... I don't really understand the wall running thing and think it would be better to just have the wall jumps/wall kick... also I still don't understand how to use the test map." Applied: on the ground the hands drop low at his sides and swing with his stride (walk and run cycles), the reaching pose stays for the air, parkour and crawling; holding an item disables grabbing (put it away with 0 to climb), replacing D-015; items are held in both hands except small ones (keys, cards, pills, phone, small devices); wall-runs are removed (M1-11 dropped) and Space by a wall is always the camera-aimed kick; a pause menu (Esc or P; it also opens when the browser drops fullscreen) lists the controls and has buttons for the movement gym, its areas and the game.
- U-006 Director: "please have it be a 3d hand model using whatever resources you can find online... scale the size to match the camera, the hands look too small... you can have the hands be visible at all times they just need to look good. Then whenever the player is holding something it can show in the hands... for items make them kind of match what the hand looks like so its seamless. Also when the player is 'sliding' on the wall they move down a little too slow and it's not supposed to slow their horizontal speed at all. Also I would like to continue testing in the development parkour area." Applied: the 2D sprites are replaced by the rigged WebXR generic hand models (MIT, @webxr-input-profiles/assets, shipped in public/models/hands, credited), posed per action from code, always visible at first-person scale with sleeves; held items become 3D models in the same style held in the right hand; the wall cling slides at 1.4 m/s and keeps all his speed along the wall; the gym stays at ?scenario=gym (also in the dev panel's scenario list and the README).
- U-005 Director: "if you are hanging off something with both hands and there is a place to push off with your feet, you should be able to do a jump again. And when you are grabbing onto something... and push the mouse down it should move your body in that direction (just a little less than white knuckle)... If you are grabbing onto a bar and there is nothing for your feet to push off of, you should be able to swing with space instead and then let go to perform a lache... make sure there's a little sliding window where you halt for a bit vertically against a wall before anything happens to give the player time to think... have the players hand against a wall or something or a sound... once the map starts getting developed, all of this should be taken into account... if the player does a short normal jump they will jump way less high then if they hold down the jump button." Applied: Space from a grip pushes off with the feet only when there's wall under them, aimed by the camera like a wall kick (look up = dyno); with no foothold (bars, beams, sign frames) Space pumps a swing and letting go mid-swing is a lache; while gripping, dragging the mouse moves the body relative to the hands (drag down hauls him up, as in White Knuckle, sideways swings him; about 70% of White Knuckle's pull; Q-005 asks whether to invert); jumping into a wall gives a 0.35 s cling where he barely slides, a palm-on-the-wall hand sprite and a soft pat sound, in which he can kick, wall-run or grab; releasing Space early cuts a jump to about 40% of the full height. The city (M3, M6) is built for these: bars and scaffolds for laches, cling walls beside gaps, footholds under lips, sill spacing that needs push-offs.
- U-004 Director: "all of the parkour movements should be integrated into the sound system... if you jump off a wall you can hear your foot kick off... the jump should be a little more vertical and less horizontal, and when you jump off a wall/run along a wall/anything involving jumping 'again' or running up a wall, you should have to use the space bar again... the direction you go should be decided by the camera... if you face up you will try to jump up... I could use one hand to redirect my momentum by grabbing something grabbable, but can't support my full weight if I'm moving a certain way fast. If I'm moving slowly I can hold on with one hand... with two hands, you can stop yourself better. It should be seamless... jumping off a wall always has the same sound so you know what you did, same with grabbing stuff, running, crawling." Applied: jump take-off 3.6 m/s (apex 0.66 m) with sprint carry trimmed to 80% so the 4.5 m gap still just clears; Space on the ground only jumps (or vaults and hops); every wall move is a second Space press in the air within 0.8 m of a wall, read from the camera: looking up at the wall kicks up it, looking along it at speed starts a wall-run, anything else kicks off in the look direction (never into the wall); two wall moves per airtime; Space during a wall-run kicks off by the camera. Grips are now a force-limited spring: momentum is kept, one hand holds his weight when slow but slips after redirecting him when fast, two hands can stop a sprint or a short fall. Every movement action emits its own sound cue (step by gait, jump, land, roll, vault, hop, slide, wall kick, wall-run step, grab, two-hand catch, slip, release, pull-over, crawl) with a fixed, recognisable synth sound (M1-31).
- U-003 Director: "some things should still require jumping, or running and then jumping and grabbing, like how real parkour works... don't edge too far into [White Knuckle] and make sure you have to use your legs too. Also the player should be able to tell when they are running or not... I don't really like stamina if it exists, I'm not sure." Applied: grab reach is tied to the body (2.3 m standing, 2.8 m with a jump, 3.9 m with a run-up), hand-over-hand only bridges holds up to about 1.15 m apart, and hand routes are spaced 1.4-2.0 m so most climbs need a jump, run-up or a leg-driven dyno; running stamina is removed (sprint, jumps and wall-runs cost nothing; low energy and injuries still slow him); only grip endurance remains, recovering whenever the hands are free, shown by the hands trembling (Q-004 asks to confirm); sprinting gets a clear FOV kick, stronger bob and lean, plus wind and footfall audio in M8.
- U-002 Director: "Whenever you want to climb something or grab onto a ledge or whatever, it should use the 'white knuckle' like hand grabbing where you click with your hand to grab and then you can mantle yourself up... you are going to have to change the way climbing works a lot... Try to mimic how white knuckle works. It will probably also require changing how the map is designed." Applied: left/right mouse grab with the left/right hand on any ledge lip, sill, pipe, rung or handhold in reach; holding hangs, the body reels toward the grip (W pulls up, S lets the arms extend), alternate hands to climb, Space from a grip launches a dyno, pull up over a lip to mantle. Grip drains stamina (one hand fast, two hands slow). Auto ledge-grab, E-attach pipes and ladders, shimmy and the 1.0-2.4 m Space mantle are replaced; Space keeps vaults and waist-high hops (up to 1.2 m), wall-runs and run-ups (which no longer auto-grab). The city generator will place hand routes (sills, pipes, AC brackets, rebar) on climbable facades and leave glass and smooth render bare. The director's sprite sheets are White Knuckle's own art and can't ship (BRIEF: third-party assets must be permissively licensed; also not reachable from the cloud session), so the hands are original code-drawn sprites in that style (Q-003).
- U-001 Director: "I really don't like the arms that come up every time you mantle... just remove them. I also wish crouch/slide was rebinded to ctrl, and it was hold to crouch, and you can get up from it during a slide animation. I also wish we could rebind the controls and change the sensitivity of the mouse (maybe add to settings later)." Applied: 3D arms removed (M1-22 dropped, replaced by the hand sprites of U-002); Ctrl (C as alternate) is hold-to-crouch, releasing it during a slide stands him up keeping some speed; key rebinding added to M8-13 settings alongside sensitivity.
- D-014 Ctrl+W closes the tab in Chrome and Edge unless the page is fullscreen with a keyboard lock, so the game asks for fullscreen and locks the keyboard when it captures the mouse; C stays as an alternate crouch key.
- D-015 (replaced by U-007) With hands on the mouse buttons, a grab took priority whenever a grabbable was in reach (stowing the held item).
- D-013 Ledge grabs use a reach band (ledge top 1.0-1.9 m above the feet) instead of hands at 2.1 m +/- 0.35: with the 0.49 m jump apex the old band let a standing jump reach 2.9 m and broke the brief's 2.4 m mantle limit. M1-10's wording updated to match; nothing weakened.
- D-012 Map authored as two 70x35 ASCII layers of 20 m tiles (type, ground level) plus hand-built set pieces and tunnel polylines; main streets are tile-wide, alleys come from splitting building tiles. Why: a real, learnable, hand-designed layout that stays cheap to edit.
- D-011 Landmark moves for play: Li Wei's construction site placed west of Jiaochangkou beside the Line 2 beam; the hospital between Jiaochangkou and Jiefangbei; Dongshuimen Bridge compressed to a 300 m span; the cableway to about 320 m; Qiansimen Bridge given a collapsed span (vantage point, not a route).
- D-010 The underground escape runs west along metro Line 1 under the Eling border wall (Yuzhong is joined to the mainland on the west), not under a river. Why: realistic geography and a land border that makes the wall meaningful.
- D-009 One spare HV fuse in the substation powers either the cableway or the metro pumps; a second sits in the tier-4 Raffles hub. Why: the shared, scarce component the brief asks for, forcing a choice.
- D-008 His father Li Guorong was an electrician at the Xinhua Road substation; his badge in the family flat is one way into it. Why: ties the story to a site and gives the player a found key with a cost (camera at the gate).
- D-007 Patrol drones fly 6 real minutes per battery and recharge 2 minutes; movement and combat run in real seconds, schedules on the game clock. Why: learnable coverage gaps at a scale the player notices.
- D-006 Unaware units' poses are pure functions of game time, so sleep is a cheap time skip and timings are exactly learnable.
- D-005 Collision is axis-aligned boxes and ramps in a uniform grid with per-axis sweeps; buildings and tunnels are carved out of solid boxes. Why: deterministic, headless, simple, fast; the city is stylized to axis-aligned blocks (decor can rotate).
- D-004 Gravity 9.81 m/s^2 and jump take-off 3.1 m/s plus 0.1 s of coyote time: at sprint a jump from the edge clears 4.5 m with a little margin and never 5.2 m.
- D-003 Puppeteer's own Chrome download works here; smoke tests use it with SwiftShader. fonts-noto-cjk was installed with apt for Chinese text in screenshots (session-only; the director's browser uses system fonts).
- D-002 Fixed tick 60 Hz; units think at 10 Hz staggered. Sim state is one plain JSON object so saves are a straight serialization.
- D-001 Pinned toolchain: TypeScript 5.9, Vite 6, Vitest 3, three.js 0.186, Puppeteer 24+. Known-stable majors over the newest.
