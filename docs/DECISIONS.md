- U-005 Director: "if you are hanging off something with both hands and there is a place to push off with your feet, you should be able to do a jump again. Applied: Space from a grip pushes off with the feet only when there's wall under them, aimed by the camera like a wall kick (look up = dyno); with no foothold (bars, beams, sign frames) Space pumps a swing and letting go mid-swing is a lache; while gripping, dragging the mouse moves the body relative to the hands (drag down hauls him up, as in White Knuckle, sideways swings him; about 70% of White Knuckle's pull; Q-005 asks whether to invert); jumping into a wall gives a 0.35 s cling where he barely slides, a palm-on-the-wall hand sprite and a soft pat sound, in which he can kick, wall-run or grab; releasing Space early cuts a jump to about 40% of the full height. The city (M3, M6) is built for these: bars and scaffolds for laches, cling walls beside gaps, footholds under lips, sill spacing that needs push-offs.
- U-004 Director: "all of the parkour movements should be integrated into the sound system... if you jump off a wall you can hear your foot kick off... the jump should be a little more vertical and less horizontal, and when you jump off a wall/run along a wall/anything involving jumping 'again' or running up a wall, you should have to use the space bar again... the direction you go should be decided by the camera... if you face up you will try to jump up... If I'm moving slowly I can hold on with one hand... with two hands, you can stop yourself better. Applied: jump take-off 3.6 m/s (apex 0.66 m) with sprint carry trimmed to 80% so the 4.5 m gap still just clears; Space on the ground only jumps (or vaults and hops); every wall move is a second Space press in the air within 0.8 m of a wall, read from the camera: looking up at the wall kicks up it, looking along it at speed starts a wall-run, anything else kicks off in the look direction (never into the wall); two wall moves per airtime; Space during a wall-run kicks off by the camera. Grips are now a force-limited spring: momentum is kept, one hand holds his weight when slow but slips after redirecting him when fast, two hands can stop a sprint or a short fall. Every movement action emits its own sound cue (step by gait, jump, land, roll, vault, hop, slide, wall kick, wall-run step, grab, two-hand catch, slip, release, pull-over, crawl) with a fixed, recognisable synth sound (M1-31).

- D-006 Unaware units' poses are pure functions of game time, so sleep is a cheap time skip and timings are exactly learnable.
- D-005 Collision is axis-aligned boxes and ramps in a uniform grid with per-axis sweeps; buildings and tunnels are carved out of solid boxes. Why: deterministic, headless, simple, fast; the city is stylized to axis-aligned blocks (decor can rotate).
- D-004 Gravity 9.81 m/s^2 and jump take-off 3.1 m/s plus 0.1 s of coyote time: at sprint a jump from the edge clears 4.5 m with a little margin and never 5.2 m.
