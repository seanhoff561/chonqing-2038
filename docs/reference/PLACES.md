# Place references

Reference photos of the real places the map models (DESIGN.md section 10). Docs only: nothing here ships in the game. Every photo is a 1280 px Wikimedia Commons thumbnail under a free licence (CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA); credits are also in CREDITS.md under "Reference photos (docs only, not shipped in the game)".

**Art rule.** The default look is `docs/reference/look-01.jpg`: abandoned, weathered, overcast, dystopian. Stained concrete and grey-brown brick, rusted window grilles, moss and weeds on flat roofs, empty window holes, a low grey sky with no hard shadows. Neon and cyberpunk light only in the formerly crowded centres: Jiefangbei, Hongyadong, Jiaochangkou and Chaotianmen, and there as dead or flickering signage over the same decay, not a clean glow. Most photos below are clean, sunny or busy tourist shots: take the geometry, materials and signage from them, never the cleanliness or the crowds.

Commons search pattern for more: `https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=<terms>`

## shibati 十八梯
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/shibati-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:十八梯老街_-_Old_Street_in_Shibati_Area_-_2015.04_-_panoramio.jpg> | rheins | CC BY 3.0 |
| `places/shibati-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shibati_十八梯_2022.1.1.jpg> | Daredemodaisuki 114514 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/shibati-3.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:原十八梯布局图.jpg> | Daredemodaisuki 114514 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Non-free links: https://www.architizer.com/projects/architecture-design-of-chongqing-shibati-traditional-style-area (2021 redevelopment design); Sze Tsung Leong, "Shibati, Yuzhong District, Chongqing", 2003 (https://phillips.com/detail/sze-tsung-leong/146140).

Notes:
- Layout (shibati-3, the original plan): not one stair but "seven streets and six lanes" (七街六巷). The main stair 十八梯老梯道 runs straight downhill through the middle; 厚慈街, 守备街, 凤凰台街, 响水桥, 下回水沟 and 花街子 cross or branch from it along the contour; lanes such as 善果巷, 月台坝 and 储奇门大巷子 thread between the house blocks. The core protected area is the dense grid of small house plots either side of the main stair. Model the stair as a spine with 3-4 contour streets crossing it and narrow lanes between.
- Before (shibati-1, 2015): worn sandstone slab steps in short flights with flat landings, cast-iron manhole covers set into them, a rough rendered retaining wall on one side and low brick shops with sagging timber eaves and corrugated awnings on the other; blue tarps, handwritten red notices, overhead wires, trees growing out of the walls.
- After (shibati-2, 2022): rebuilt rows of 2-3 floor houses with grey tile pitched roofs, white render and grey brick, dark timber trim, red lanterns; the stair is wide and packed with people; 20-40 floor towers and tower cranes loom above at Jiaochangkou in the haze. The 2038 Shibati is this rebuild gone derelict.
- Materials and colours: grey and tan brick, grey render with black rain streaks, dark timber, grey tile, worn yellow-grey sandstone steps, rusted iron; red lanterns and paper notices as the only warm colour.
- Signage: carved wooden shop boards, vertical hanging signs, handwritten red-paper notices taped to walls.
- Play: the main stair has long sight lines downhill (dangerous to cross, easy to watch); the contour streets are flatter side routes; house rows are a parallel rooftop route stepping down (one ridge height per row, 2-2.5 m jumps); the eave lips, awnings and retaining walls give the climbs.

## jiaochangkou 较场口
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/jiaochangkou-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:重庆较场口的著名建筑（符合环保理念）2009.03.21_-_panoramio.jpg> | 杨志强Zhiqiang | CC BY-SA 3.0 |
| `places/jiaochangkou-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:较场口2_地面.jpg> | 重庆轨交18 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Notes:
- jiaochangkou-1: an old 3-4 storey timber-frame house with board walls, open balconies, a deep tile roof and an AC unit bolted on, squeezed between tile-clad 1990s slab blocks: the old city survives in the gaps of the new. Good model for a hand-built set piece near the junction.
- jiaochangkou-2: a metro entrance cut into the base of the "SML Center" (日月光中心) block: granite slab cladding, a red-and-white sign box with the Line 1 and Line 2 icons and the station name, the green CRT logo on a projecting blade, a purple gym banner, a convenience store with drinks stacked out front, escalators going down behind glass doors.
- Layout: a junction on the ridge (about 40 m) where Minzu Road, Zhongxing Road and the road down to Shibati meet; overpasses and underpasses, the head of the Shibati steps, the Grand Tunnel memorial just below.
- Line 2 at Jiaochangkou is underground: the terminus platform (monorail-line2-3) sits inside the ridge, and the beam emerges from the hillside to the west towards Linjiangmen. The game's elevated beam should leave the ridge through a portal in the retaining wall.
- Materials: grey granite cladding, white and pink tile facades streaked grey, glass shopfronts, steel railings.
- Neon allowed: light-box sign bands over every shopfront, LED tickers; in 2038 dead or flickering, sign boxes cracked.
- Play: an open hub with cross-fire from overpasses; station entrances and underpasses as escape routes into the ground; the ridge edge to drop or climb towards Shibati.

## monorail-line2 重庆轨道交通2号线
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/monorail-line2-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chongqing_Rail_Transit_Line_2_Monorail_running_along_Jialing_River.jpg> | Juukeihc at English Wikipedia | CC BY-SA 3.0 |
| `places/monorail-line2-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Liziba_Station_with_02031_(20191224152311).jpg> | N509FZ | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/monorail-line2-3.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Platform_of_Jiaochangkou_Station,_2017-09-22.jpg> | Siyuwj | CC BY-SA 3.0 |

Notes:
- Structure (monorail-line2-2, Liziba): a pair of narrow concrete guideway beams on tall single concrete columns with T-shaped crossheads, 10-30 m up, curving along the steep hillside; the columns step down with the slope so the beam stays level. Residential towers and a building wrapped in green safety netting stand right beside it.
- monorail-line2-1: at dusk along the Jialing, the beam runs on a viaduct with low concrete side walls and lamp posts, a 4-car train with a red-brown stripe; city towers across the water.
- monorail-line2-3: Jiaochangkou platform (2/01): platform screen doors, green Line 2 stripe and route diagram, aluminium slat ceiling, polished stone floor; underground in the ridge.
- Trains: four-car rubber-tyred straddle monorail (Hitachi type), boxy with rounded ends; in 2038 one stranded on the beam is a strong set piece.
- Materials: grey precast beams with rust streaks under the joints, steel finger joints, cable trays along the sides.
- Play: the beam is a high, exposed balance route (gaps at joints, cable trays to hang from) linking Jiaochangkou to the construction site; columns climbable at maintenance ladders; anyone on it is visible from far off.

## jiefangbei 解放碑
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/jiefangbei-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jiefangbei_(People's_Liberation_Monument).jpg> | Juukeihc | CC BY-SA 3.0 |
| `places/jiefangbei-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jiefangbei_CBD_20240721.jpg> | HoweyYuan | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/jiefangbei-3.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jiefangbei_night.jpg> | Baycrest | CC BY-SA 2.5 |

Notes:
- Monument (jiefangbei-1, -3): a slim octagonal pale stone tower about 27.5 m tall on a stepped base, clock faces near the top, a small balustraded lantern and a thin spire with a vane; dwarfed by the glass towers around it.
- Layout (jiefangbei-2): wide pedestrian streets paved in a grey and buff granite grid run straight into the monument plaza; tall decorative lamp posts with clusters of globe lamps; malls with green and gold glass on both sides, the WFC tower (pointed crown) and other 40-70 floor towers behind.
- Night (jiefangbei-3): huge LED billboards on mall corners (one wrapping a curved corner), luxury shopfronts, lit tower tops; the plaza is a bowl of light.
- Materials: glass curtain walls (blue-green, gold), polished granite, steel and aluminium cladding, LED panels.
- Neon allowed: the densest LED district; in 2038 dead screens with a few still looping, cracked glass, dead escalators.
- Play: the open plaza is a killing ground overlooked by towers; malls give indoor routes, escalator wells and service corridors; the monument is the orientation landmark and a perch at the clock.

## hongyadong 洪崖洞
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/hongyadong-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hongya_Cave_20180520.jpg> | xiquinhosilva | CC BY 2.0 |
| `places/hongyadong-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:202308_Hongya_Cave_at_night_from_Qiansimen_Bridge.jpg> | Jonashtand | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/hongyadong-3.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hongya_Cave_interior_20240722.jpg> | HoweyYuan | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Notes:
- Structure (hongyadong-1): about 11 storeys of dark timber-clad galleries, stacked roofs and balconies built against the Jialing cliff, with 30-50 floor towers right above and behind it. At its foot the riverside road runs on a concrete viaduct on columns, with car parks underneath; the orange steel truss of the Qiansimen Bridge approach passes alongside at mid height.
- Night (hongyadong-2): every eave outlined in warm gold light, tiered roofs like a stepped mountain, the road below a river of traffic. In 2038: most strips dead, some flickering.
- Inside (hongyadong-3): the bare cliff rock face is the back wall of the inner streets; vertical neon and LED signs stacked at every level, open stairs and galleries between floors, ducts and pipes running overhead, plastic flower displays.
- Materials: dark stained timber posts and lattice over a concrete frame, grey tile roofs with upturned corners, red lanterns, raw rock.
- Neon allowed (it was a crowded centre).
- Play: strong verticality: climb the outer galleries floor by floor, drop eave to eave, or use the inner stairs; entrances at 44, 30 and 12 m link upper city to riverfront; the rock wall inside is a hidden climb; view of the Qiansimen Bridge.

## xinhua-road 新华路
No usable free photos found: Commons results for "Xinhua Road" / 新华路 were of the same-named roads in other cities. The cableway photos (cableway-1) show the Yangtze side below it. Linked (map, not free photos): https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=%E6%96%B0%E5%8D%8E%E8%B7%AF%20%E6%B8%9D%E4%B8%AD%E5%8C%BA

Notes (general knowledge, check against real photos if found):
- Layout: a curving road along the Yangtze side of the peninsula below the CBD, lined with 6-20 floor buildings, with steep alleys and stairs climbing to Jiefangbei above.
- Character: ordinary lower city, older than the CBD: 1980s-90s tile-clad blocks, ground-floor wholesale shops, small hotels, the cableway station, the riverside below.
- Materials: off-white and pink small square tiles with rust and rain streaks, aluminium windows, AC units on every bay, steel security grilles on balconies.
- Elevation: about 10-20 m, with retaining walls against the hill on the uphill side; buildings whose roofs meet the street above.
- Play: a mid-height corridor between riverfront and CBD; grilles and AC units are climbable; stair alleys are chokepoints; the substation is a fenced compound with transformers for cover.

| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|

Notes:
- xiaoshizi-1: Line 1 platform: screen doors, red line stripe, aluminium slat ceiling, a yellow exit sign and a "transfer to Line 6" sign by an escalator in a white column. Model the station control room and transfer passages in this clean white-and-steel style, gone dark.
- xiaoshizi-2: Exit 4B: a straight stair dropping from the pavement into a concrete trough with steel handrails and a canopy-less mouth, a green CRT totem with "地铁出入口" and an arrow; office towers of 20-40 floors around, street trees, blue construction hoarding.

## chaotianmen 朝天门 and Raffles City / The Crystal
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/chaotianmen-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:重慶_朝天門_Chaotianmen_in_Chongqing.jpg> | LowensteinYang | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/chaotianmen-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raffles_City_Chongqing_from_Yangtze_River.jpg> | Junyi Lou | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/chaotianmen-3.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raffles_City_Chongqing_20260507-1.jpg> | Suicasmo | CC0 |

Notes:
- Chaotianmen (chaotianmen-1): a curved, terraced square building with the huge red characters 朝天门 on its roof, a band of blue glass, stone-clad tiers with railings stepping down towards the river, flood lamps on poles; overcast sky.
- Raffles City (chaotianmen-2): eight towers in two curving rows; the two tallest (about 250 m) stand free on the very tip, and The Crystal, a horizontal glass-and-steel conservatory, lies across the tops of four towers behind them; the towers stand on a long podium mall that meets the river at the docks. Across the Jialing, the green glass Grand Theatre.
- Night (chaotianmen-3): towers striped with vertical red light bands and lit crowns; the podium a band of shop light.
- Materials: pale grey stone and vertical glass louvres on the towers, a gridded glass and steel conservatory, concrete terraces and steel piers at the water.
- Neon allowed: towers and The Crystal mostly dark in 2038, a few panels lit, glass missing.
- Play: the tip is the map's end and a high-value set piece; climbing a tower to The Crystal is a long vertical route; piers and the river-police dock offer a boat exit; the two rivers meet below (Jialing greener, Yangtze browner).

## dongshuimen-bridge 东水门大桥
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/dongshuimen-bridge-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dongshuimen_Yangtze_River_Bridge_2021-07-27.jpg> | JamesYoung8167 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/dongshuimen-bridge-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dongshuimen_Bridge_20251028_(1).jpg> | DvTor8303 | CC0 |

Notes:
- Structure: a double-deck cable-stayed bridge with two white spindle-shaped towers (two legs that bulge apart and close to a point at the top), one near each bank, and a fan of orange cables to each side.
- The girder is an orange-red steel Warren truss: road on top, Line 6 rails inside the truss.
- Surroundings (dongshuimen-bridge-1): on the peninsula side the riverside road with street trees, below the grey tile roofs of the Huguang Guild Hall; barges on a brown Yangtze; Nan'an's green hills with tower blocks on the far bank.
- Play: road deck at 40 m and rail deck at 32 m inside the truss give two parallel crossings with truss members as cover and climbs between them; the obvious checkpoint on the route south.

## riverfronts 嘉陵江 / 长江 滨江
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/riverfronts-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:重庆市渝中区半岛.jpg> | 夏枫水月 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Notes:
- riverfronts-1: the peninsula seen across water: a dense wall of 30-60 floor towers ending at Chaotianmen, the Qiansimen tower on the left, sky reflected in a still foreground pool.
- riverfronts-2: the green Jialing seen from high up: a long road viaduct and a monorail bridge on blue-painted piers crossing the river, interchange ramps looping down to the bank, a station built over the riverside road, bare mud and construction on the shore.

## air-raid-tunnels 防空洞 / Grand Tunnel 大隧道
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/air-raid-tunnels-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:十八梯大隧道遗址_2022.1.1.jpg> | Daredemodaisuki 114514 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/air-raid-tunnels-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bombing_of_Chongqing_20180501_085524.jpg> | Qa003qa003 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `places/air-raid-tunnels-3.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bombing_of_Chongqing_20180501_085315.jpg> | Qa003qa003 | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Background: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombing_of_Chongqing (the 1941 Grand Tunnel disaster).

Notes:
- Entrance (air-raid-tunnels-1): the Grand Tunnel site at Shibati is a round-arched opening about 3 m wide in a cream rendered retaining wall, the name 十八梯大隧道遗址 carved above it, a balustraded terrace and rebuilt traditional houses on top. The tunnel mouth is dark straight away.
- Memorial (air-raid-tunnels-2): a stone relief frieze of crowded figures above a wall of grey dressed stone blocks with a black inscribed plaque.
- Inside the mouth (air-raid-tunnels-3): red-brick lining, a black iron railing and notices "参观止步 洞内设施不完善" (no entry, facilities incomplete); beyond is rock.
- Form: tunnels cut in red-brown sandstone, about 2-4 m wide and 2-3 m high, arched or squared, plastered, brick-lined or bare rock; mouths are low arches in retaining walls, often gated. Many were reused as hotpot restaurants, stores and garages.
- Play: the underground network linking districts (hospital basement, telecom cable tunnel); dark, damp, echoing, short sight lines; mouths in retaining walls are hidden exits.

## old-housing 老旧住宅 (Yuzhong old blocks)
| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `places/old-housing-1.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:十八梯一楼一底的民居_-_panoramio.jpg> | 杨志强Zhiqiang | CC BY-SA 3.0 |
| `places/old-housing-2.jpg` | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:碳排放为领先水平的十八梯民居_-_panoramio.jpg> | 杨志强Zhiqiang | CC BY-SA 3.0 |

Also see look-01.jpg (the main mood reference) and jiaochangkou-1 (timber house among slab blocks).

Notes:
- Low-rise (old-housing-1): a brick ground floor with a rendered upper floor, an external timber stair with a handrail climbing the front to the upper door, laundry hanging from wires, a red fire-hydrant sign, a narrow concrete alley; plaster flaking off the brick.
- Roofscape (old-housing-2, old Shibati): a sea of grey clay-tile and grey corrugated asbestos-sheet roofs at many heights, timber-board and brick walls, laundry and small windows, broken and patched roofs, trees growing out of the gaps; 5-7 floor brick walk-ups behind. Exactly the rooftop route the design wants.
- Walk-ups (look-01): 5-9 floor concrete slab blocks, grey-brown with black water staining from every ledge, small windows, many empty holes, balconies with breeze-block or lattice parapets, pierced-brick panels on stair towers.
- High-rises behind: 15-30 floor towers clad in white or pink small tiles with horizontal banding, AC units and barred windows on each bay.
- Density: buildings butt against each other at different heights with no gaps, so roofs at one level meet windows of the next; drops of one to three floors between neighbours.
- Colour: desaturated tan, grey and dull pink; spots of colour only from blossom, laundry and red paper; flat overcast light.
- Play: the core of the climbable fabric: external stairs, balconies, window holes and AC units as hand holds, roofs as a stepped rooftop route, empty interiors to cut through.

- Stairwells and corridors (interior-1): bare concrete dog-leg stair with worn grey-white edges, a steel baluster rail with a red-painted handrail, painted walls (white over a grey-green dado), electricity meter boxes and cable runs on the walls, a dim corridor running off the landing with doors on one side. In the walk-ups: one stair per block, landings every half-floor, a window or breeze-block panel on each landing.
- Flat doors (interior-2): heavy steel "security doors" in imitation woodgrain with a brass lever and peephole, a timber frame, terrazzo or speckled tile floor in the common hall; inside, a short hallway with built-in cabinets, coat hooks and a mirror.
- Kitchens (interior-3): a tiny galley with a single window, chequered beige-and-brown wall tiles, red-brown laminate cabinets with arched panels, a black worktop, a two-ring gas hob and a slanted range hood, plastic bags and bottles everywhere. Props: gas bottle, rice cooker, wok, thermos, cleaver, chopping block.
- Living rooms (interior-4): one room does everything: a big fabric sofa, a bed or massage table, a desk at the window, a fridge, plastic stools, a ceiling lamp, a wall of aluminium windows; ochre or cream painted walls, laminate or tiled floor. In 2038: furniture shoved against doors, mattresses on the floor, mildew blooms on the walls.
- Derelict floors (interior-5): a concrete slab floor caked in grey dust, a heap of collapsed ceiling boards and timber in the middle, broken cabinets and sideboards, a torn ceiling with dangling battens, the outer wall gone so the room opens straight onto a balcony rail and fog. Good model for cover piles and for floors with holes.
- Ruins (interior-6): a two-storey brick shell with no roof or floors left, window holes in rows, the whole thing green with moss, creepers and ferns, trees growing inside, leaf litter on the ground floor. Use for long-abandoned blocks on the city edge.
- Air-raid tunnels (interior-7, -8): arched tunnels in sprayed or rendered concrete over rock, about 3 m wide, a flat concrete floor, cable trays and pipes along the crown, rows of small lamps, side rooms opening off the main bore, painted signs on boards and wall-painted plans. In Chongqing the same tunnels hold hotpot tables, store goods or sit flooded and dark.
- Hotpot restaurants (interior-9): round tables with a sunken divided "yuanyang" pot (red chilli broth and clear broth) in the middle, a lazy susan, plates of raw meat and tofu skin around it, padded high-backed chairs; tiled floors, extractor ducts, gas cylinders under the tables. In 2038: congealed red oil, overturned chairs, the gas bottles a hazard or a tool.
- Shops and stalls (interior-10, Chongqing 1983): open-fronted ground floors under corrugated and canvas awnings on poles, roast ducks hanging over the counter, hand-painted signs on white boards, grimy plastered walls behind. Shops are a single deep room with a roll-up shutter, the owners living in the back or upstairs.
