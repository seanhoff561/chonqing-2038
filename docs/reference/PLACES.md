# Place references

Reference material for the real places the map models (DESIGN.md section 10). Docs only, nothing here ships in the game.

**Art rule.** The default look is `docs/reference/look-01.jpg`: abandoned, weathered, overcast, dystopian. Stained concrete and grey-brown brick, rusted window grilles, moss and weeds on flat roofs, empty window holes, a low grey sky with no hard shadows. Neon and cyberpunk light only in the formerly crowded centres: Jiefangbei, Hongyadong, Jiaochangkou and Chaotianmen, and there as dead or flickering signage over the same decay, not a clean glow.

## shibati 十八梯
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Shibati+Chongqing ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=%E5%8D%81%E5%85%AB%E6%A2%AF
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shibati (article text) ; https://www.architizer.com/projects/architecture-design-of-chongqing-shibati-traditional-style-area (2021 redevelopment design) ; Sze Tsung Leong, "Shibati, Yuzhong District, Chongqing" 2003 (https://phillips.com/detail/sze-tsung-leong/146140)

Notes:
- Layout: one stone stair street falling from Jiaochangkou on the ridge (about 40 m) south-east towards the Yangtze (about 14 m), with short flat landings and side alleys branching off along the contour. The name means "eighteen steps"; the real descent is hundreds of steps in flights of 10-20.
- Before (to 2010s): a dense hillside of 2-6 floor brick and timber houses stacked on top of one another, with 7-15 floor concrete slab blocks behind and above (look-01.jpg is exactly this): flat roofs with moss, rubble and shacks, roofs of one row level with doorways of the row above.
- After (2021 rebuild): new "traditional" style rows, 2-3 floors, grey brick, dark timber frames and lattice, deep wooden eaves and grey tile pitched roofs, granite steps and stone-slab landings, lanterns. The game's 2038 Shibati is this rebuild gone derelict: eaves sagging, lattice broken, shop shutters down, weeds in the stair joints.
- Materials and colours: grey and tan brick, black-stained timber, blue-grey roof tile, worn granite steps, rusted iron rails; touches of faded red (lanterns, couplets) as the only warm colour.
- Signage: carved wooden shop boards and vertical hanging signs, hotpot and noodle shops, handwritten paper notices; old Shibati walls carried painted 拆 (demolish) marks.
- Play: the stair street is the main north-south route with long sight lines down it (dangerous to cross); the house rows beside it are a parallel rooftop route (one ridge height per row, 2-2.5 m jumps); side alleys and doorway recesses give short cover; the drop between rows makes ledges, eave lips and wall climbs.

## jiaochangkou 较场口
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Jiaochangkou ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=%E8%BE%83%E5%9C%BA%E5%8F%A3
- Non-free links: https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%BE%83%E5%9C%BA%E5%8F%A3%E7%AB%99

Notes:
- Layout: a busy roundabout-style junction on the ridge where Minzu Road, Zhongxing Road and the road down to Shibati meet; pedestrian overpasses and underpasses, a small plaza at the head of the Shibati steps.
- Elevation: on the upper city (about 40 m); the ground falls away steeply south to Shibati and the Yangtze, so the junction edge is a retaining wall with a view over the lower city.
- Landmarks: the Line 2 terminus building and its elevated beam; the Grand Tunnel (大隧道) memorial entrance near the junction; 1990s-2000s tile-clad towers with ground-floor shops.
- Materials: white and pink ceramic-tile facades streaked grey, glass shopfronts, steel overpass railings, bus shelters.
- Signage (neon allowed): big shop and pharmacy light boxes, LED tickers over the junction, now dead or flickering.
- Play: an open hub with cross-fire from overpasses and the monorail beam; underpasses and the tunnel mouth as escape routes; the retaining-wall edge to drop or climb towards Shibati.

## monorail-line2 重庆轨道交通2号线 (beam and Jiaochangkou terminus)
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Chongqing+Rail+Transit+Line+2 ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Jiaochangkou+station
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_2_(Chongqing_Rail_Transit)

Notes:
- Structure: straddle-beam monorail (Hitachi type): a single concrete guideway beam about 0.85 m wide and 1.5 m deep on T- or portal-shaped concrete piers, running at 8-20 m above the street and hugging the hillside along the Jialing side west of Jiaochangkou.
- Terminus: Jiaochangkou is the eastern end; the line comes out of the hill/structure and the station is enclosed, with platforms either side of the beams and a short tail track.
- Trains: four-car rubber-tyred cars, rounded fronts, white/red or white/blue livery; in 2038 one stranded train on the beam is a strong set piece.
- Materials: grey precast beam with rust streaks below joints, steel finger joints, cable trays along the beam sides, a thin service walkway on some spans.
- Play: the beam is a high, exposed balance route (a gap to jump at joints, cable trays to hang from) linking Jiaochangkou to the construction site; piers are climbable at maintenance ladders; anything on the beam is visible from far away.

## jiefangbei 解放碑
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Jiefangbei ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=People%27s+Liberation+Monument+Chongqing
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_to_the_People%27s_Liberation

Notes:
- Monument: an octagonal pillar about 27.5 m tall, pale grey/cream stone with a clock face near the top and a small balustraded crown, on a stepped plinth in the middle of a paved pedestrian plaza.
- Layout: a pedestrian cross of wide paved streets (Minquan Road, Minzu Road, Zourong Road) radiating from the plaza, walled in by malls and 30-50 floor towers; the plaza sits on the flat ridge top (about 40-45 m).
- Materials: polished granite paving, glass curtain walls, steel and aluminium cladding, giant LED screens on mall facades, planters and benches.
- Neon allowed: the densest LED and neon district, now dead screens with a few still looping, cracked glass, dead escalators.
- Play: wide open plaza is a killing ground watched from towers; malls give indoor routes, escalator wells and service corridors; the monument is a central landmark for orientation and a sniper perch at the clock.

## hongyadong 洪崖洞
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Hongyadong
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongyadong

Notes:
- Structure: an 11-floor diaojiaolou (stilt-house) style complex stacked down the Jialing cliff, about 70 m of height from the riverside road to Cangbai Road at the top; each level has its own street entrance, and the top floor is a street at ground level.
- Look: dark timber posts and beams, upturned grey tile eaves stacked in tiers, balconies and galleries all round, red lanterns, with a concrete frame behind the timber.
- Neon allowed: famous for warm gold floodlighting and lanterns at night; in 2038 half dead, gold strips flickering over black wood, lanterns torn.
- Inside: narrow shopping lanes on each level, lifts and stair cores linking levels, open galleries overlooking the river.
- Play: strong verticality: climb the outside galleries floor by floor, drop from eave to eave, or take the stair cores; entrances at 44, 30 and 12 m link upper city to riverfront; good vantage on Qiansimen Bridge.

## xinhua-road 新华路
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Xinhua+Road+Chongqing ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=%E6%96%B0%E5%8D%8E%E8%B7%AF+%E9%87%8D%E5%BA%86

Notes:
- Layout: a curving road along the Yangtze side of the peninsula below the CBD, lined with 6-20 floor buildings, with steep alleys and stairs climbing to Jiefangbei above.
- Character: ordinary lower city, older than the CBD: 1980s-90s tile-clad blocks, ground-floor wholesale shops, small hotels, the cableway station at Xinhua Road and the riverside below.
- Materials: off-white and pink small square tiles with rust and rain streaks, aluminium windows, AC units on every bay, steel security grilles on balconies.
- Elevation: about 10-20 m, with retaining walls against the hill on the uphill side; buildings whose roofs meet the street above.
- Play: a mid-height corridor between riverfront and CBD; grilles and AC units are climbable; stair alleys are chokepoints; the substation is a fenced compound with transformers for cover.

## xiaoshizi 小什字
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Xiaoshizi ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=%E5%B0%8F%E4%BB%80%E5%AD%97

Notes:
- Layout: a crossroads near the tip, between Jiefangbei and Chaotianmen, where Line 1 and Line 6 interchange; the Dongshuimen Bridge approach ramps start nearby.
- Landmarks: deep metro station entrances (long escalators down into the hill), Luohan Temple (罗汉寺) nearby, bank and hotel towers.
- Materials: granite, glass and steel station canopies; older brick buildings in side lanes.
- Play: the metro entrance and control room are the way into the underground; bridge approach ramps give elevated overpass routes across the district.

## chaotianmen 朝天门 and Raffles City / The Crystal
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Chaotianmen ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Raffles+City+Chongqing
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raffles_City_Chongqing ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaotianmen

Notes:
- Raffles City: eight towers (Safdie Architects, 2019) in two curving rows like sails, the two tallest about 250 m on the tip; The Crystal is a horizontal glass conservatory about 300 m long bridging four towers at about 250 m, its two ends cantilevered.
- Chaotianmen: the tip of the peninsula where the Jialing (greener) meets the Yangtze (browner), with a broad terraced plaza, steps down to river cruise piers and floating docks.
- Materials: pale stone and glass louvres on the towers, a gridded glass and steel conservatory; concrete terraces and steel piers at the water.
- Neon allowed: towers and The Crystal light shows; in 2038 mostly dark, a few panels lit, glass panes missing.
- Play: the tip is the end of the map and a high-value set piece; climbing the towers to The Crystal is a long vertical route; piers and the river-police dock offer a boat exit; the two-colour river meeting is the vista.

## dongshuimen-bridge 东水门大桥
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Dongshuimen+Bridge
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongshuimen_Bridge

Notes:
- Structure: a double-deck cable-stayed bridge across the Yangtze (2014): two diamond/spindle-shaped single towers, a steel truss girder with the road on top and Line 6 rails inside the truss.
- Colours: steel truss painted grey-white; towers concrete; fan of stay cables.
- Play: road deck at 40 m, rail deck at 32 m inside the truss: two parallel crossings with the truss members as cover and climbing between them; the bridge is the main route south and an obvious checkpoint.

## qiansimen-bridge 千厮门大桥
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Qiansimen+Bridge
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiansimen_Bridge

Notes:
- Structure: twin of Dongshuimen on the Jialing side, also double-deck cable-stayed with a single spindle tower, landing near Hongyadong.
- In the game: sealed by a collapsed span, so a vantage point. Model a torn truss end hanging towards the river, cables slack, and the deck as a lookout over Hongyadong and the Jialing.

## cableway 长江索道
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Yangtze+River+Cableway
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangtze_River_Cableway

Notes:
- Structure: an aerial tramway (1987) about 1.2 km across the Yangtze from Xinhua Road to Nan'an, two large cabins running in opposite directions on fixed track ropes, no intermediate towers.
- Stations: concrete station houses wedged into the hillside, cabin docks open to the river side, machinery room with large bullwheels.
- Cabins: boxy, red/orange or blue-white with large windows, about 80 people each.
- Play: one cabin stuck mid-river is a set piece; the station's machine room and the cabin roof (track rope hanging) give a risky crossing; the station is a lookout over the Yangtze.

## riverfronts 嘉陵江 / 长江 滨江
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Yuzhong+riverside+Chongqing ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Jialing+River+Chongqing

Notes:
- Layout: riverside expressways (Binjiang Road) on embankments 4-8 m above the river, with high concrete flood walls and stepped embankments down to a muddy shore; the city rises abruptly behind as cliff and retaining walls.
- Levels: water level varies by about 30 m over a year (Three Gorges reservoir), leaving mud banks, exposed steps and stranded pontoons at low water.
- Materials: stained concrete, rubble-stone revetments, steel floating docks and gangways, rusted barges.
- Play: the game adds prefab barrier walls along the road; docks and pontoons are boat access; the embankment steps and drains are hidden routes under the road.

## air-raid-tunnels 防空洞 / Grand Tunnel 大隧道
- Images: none downloaded.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Chongqing+air+raid+shelter ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=%E5%A4%A7%E9%9A%A7%E9%81%93
- Non-free links: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqing_Tunnel_Massacre

Notes:
- History: the 1941 Grand Tunnel disaster site at Jiaochangkou is a memorial with the original tunnel mouth; hundreds of WWII shelters riddle the sandstone of the peninsula.
- Form: tunnels cut in red-brown sandstone, about 2-4 m wide and 2-3 m high, arched or squared, plastered or bare rock; mouths are low arched doorways in retaining walls, often with iron gates.
- Reuse: many became hotpot restaurants, storage, garages, cool halls in summer, so there are strip lights, cables on the walls, tiled floors and partitions.
- Materials: damp rock with water staining, moss at the mouths, rusted gates, exposed wiring.
- Play: the underground network linking districts (hospital basement, telecom cable tunnel); dark, echoing, short sight lines; mouths in retaining walls are hidden exits.

## old-housing 老旧住宅 (Yuzhong slab blocks)
- Images: none downloaded. Main reference is look-01.jpg.
- Free source search: https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Yuzhong+District+residential+building ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image&search=Chongqing+old+apartment

Notes (from look-01.jpg):
- Walk-ups: 5-9 floor concrete slab blocks, grey-brown with black water staining from every ledge, small square windows, many empty holes, balconies with breeze-block or lattice parapets, decorative pierced-brick panels on stair towers.
- Low-rises: 2-4 floor grey-tan brick boxes stacked down the slope, flat roofs with moss, weeds, small trees and corrugated sheds; some roofs patched with blue metal sheet.
- High-rises behind: 15-30 floor towers clad in white or pink small tiles with horizontal banding, AC units and barred windows in each bay, glass block stair strips.
- Density: buildings butt against each other at different heights with no gaps, so roofs at one level meet windows of the next; drop heights of one to three floors between neighbours.
- Colour: desaturated tan, grey and dull pink; a few spots of colour from blossom, laundry and a red banner; flat overcast light, no sky blue.
- Play: the core of the climbable fabric: balcony ledges, window holes and AC units as hand holds, flat roofs as a rooftop route stepped down the hill, empty interiors to cut through.
